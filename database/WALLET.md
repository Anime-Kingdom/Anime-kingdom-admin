# Partner wallet
Raggo receives 10% of subtotal minus discount, rounded to paise and excluding shipping. The administrator must mark Delivered to credit earnings. Repeat updates cannot duplicate credit. Code ownership and the rate are stored per order. Raw tables and QR images are private. Partners see their own order references and earnings, without customer contact details.
Withdrawals require INR1,000, reserve funds and permit one open request. Retried request keys cannot create duplicates. The creator uploads a QR and payee name for private admin review. The administrator pays outside the site and records a transaction reference. Marking Paid does not transfer money. Rejected requests release the reservation. Resolved requests cannot be paid again. Reversed, cancelled or deleted orders remove credit; reversals after payment carry forward as a negative balance. Payment recording rechecks eligible earnings.
Creator partners controls login access; Partner wallets reviews requests and attribution; Order dispatch controls delivery. Coupons manage customer discounts independently. Future creator rates default to zero; only Raggo has the authorized 10% arrangement. Historical orders require explicit delivery confirmation.
The wallet migration is installed. Verification uses synthetic orders, product and creator in a rolled-back transaction. No payment is executed. Tests cover calculation, status gating, duplicate protection, reservation, minimum and balance checks, rejection, paid accounting, reversals and privacy.
